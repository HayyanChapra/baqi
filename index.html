Before you ask

Do I pay in the app?

Yes. You pay in the app when you reserve. In this prototype the payment page is a demo and no money moves.

Are these real restaurants?

No. The map is real, but every kitchen and dish on Baqi is imaginary, made for a class project.

What if I cannot make my pickup?

Contact support and they will guide you. You can reach them from the Contact page.

Why is there no surprise box?

Baqi shows you the actual dish before you reserve it. No lucky dip.

Why Baqi exists

Every evening, kitchens across Dubai close with perfectly good food left on the counter, while students a few streets away skip dinner or settle for whatever is cheapest. Baqi connects the two, and lets students choose the plate they actually want instead of a mystery box.

Mission

Make good food affordable for students by giving surplus meals a second chance.

Vision

Every campus in the UAE eats well and wastes nothing.

Values

Choice, fairness to restaurants, and zero waste.
